A bit about myself

I am a product designer who loves what design brings to the community, with a strong passions for food and cooking. I entered both worlds being very young.

when I was 11, I started playing around with food and every single dish that I created took time and dedication. By combining different colours and textures I was already designing something very consciously, and I was always thinking of myself as the consumer of that dish, so it was #user-centric ;) 

By the age of 12, I learnt basic HTML at school. This was another scenario where I could be in control of the design of something, and I loved it! With this, a hobby was born: every day after school I went back home and started a new web of whatever theme or subject. Opening a bloc of notes and starting to code was the best part of those days #nerd

This was a clear door towards entering digital design some years later. I am lucky that this path was marked so many years ago and I still love to follow it every day :)
