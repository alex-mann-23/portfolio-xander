About myself

I am a product designer that loves what design has to bring for the community, with a strong passions for food 🍔

I entered both worlds when I was a child. My passion for food came first. I love to cook since I was 10 and every single dish that I created took time and dedication. By combining different colours and textures I was already designing something very consciously.  I was always thinking of myself as the consumer of that dish and I was trying to make it so as to perfectly suit my desire.

Later on, in primary school, I was introduced to the world of HTML. That was another scenario where I could, once again, choose all the aspects of the design of something. I immediately found that exciting and I loved it.  With this, another hobby was born: every day after school I went back home and started a new web of whatever theme or subject. Opening a bloc of notes and starting to code was the best part of those days #nerd

This was a clear door towards entering digital design some years later. I am lucky that this path was marked so many years ago and I still love to follow it every day!
